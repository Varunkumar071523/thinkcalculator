# ThinkCalculator Roadmap

The roadmap is phased by capability and exit criteria, not arbitrary dates. Current status is maintained in [PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md).

## Phase 1 — Platform Foundation — Complete

- Objective: establish a production-oriented, testable calculator and content platform.
- Deliverables: project setup, design system, shared calculator framework, five finance calculators, homepage and navigation, URL sharing, copy/print actions, charts, schedules, automated tests, SEO, production-readiness features, calculator knowledge content, and Blog/Guide engine.
- Exit criteria: the five calculators and four editorial items build statically, registries exclude drafts, and test/lint/build checks pass.
- Dependencies: Next.js application toolchain and version-controlled content.
- Out of scope: live deployment and ongoing content expansion.

## Phase 2 — Topic Authority — Complete

- Objective: connect calculators with a useful, navigable learning layer.
- Deliverables: Glossary Engine, topic/category hubs, editorial search, expanded reciprocal internal links, and focused content clusters.
- Exit criteria: met in Sprint 15. Public glossary and hub routes have substantive content, metadata, accurate schema, tests, sitemap coverage, linked learning paths, and reciprocal contextual navigation; the stable search route backs the existing SearchAction.
- Dependencies: current editorial taxonomy and registry conventions; editorial review capacity.
- Out of scope: mass-generated articles, CMS migration, and personalised recommendations.

Continued publication within the existing engines is ongoing editorial work, not an unfinished Phase 2 platform capability. Savings stays non-public until its canonical registries contain the same substantive mix required of Loans and Investing.

## Phase 3 — Calculator Expansion — Next

- Objective: broaden practical finance and business coverage without weakening validation quality.
- Deliverables: candidates include CAGR, PPF, GST, gratuity, step-up SIP, SWP, inflation, retirement, income tax, and HRA calculators.
- Exit criteria: each selected calculator has typed inputs/results, pure logic, validation, tests, content, SEO, accessibility, and related links.
- Dependencies: verified formulas and reliable official sources for regulatory rules.
- Out of scope: launching every candidate at once or publishing unstable tax logic without a maintenance plan.

## Phase 4 — Public Launch

- Objective: deploy and verify the platform in its real production environment.
- Deliverables: Hostinger deployment, domain and HTTPS verification, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, privacy-reviewed analytics, sitemap submission, Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals review, and backup/rollback validation.
- Exit criteria: production smoke tests pass, monitoring and recovery ownership are documented, and live-domain metadata and headers are verified.
- Dependencies: hosting access, DNS control, webmaster accounts, analytics decision, and operational ownership.
- Out of scope: monetisation before reliability and policy review.

## Phase 5 — Monetization

- Objective: evaluate sustainable revenue without compromising trust or usability.
- Deliverables: AdSense evaluation, affiliate-content rules, sponsored-content policy, and newsletter backend if approved.
- Exit criteria: disclosures, privacy/consent, performance impact, editorial independence, and monitoring are approved.
- Dependencies: stable traffic, public policies, legal/privacy review, and live analytics.
- Out of scope: undisclosed placement, deceptive recommendations, or guaranteed revenue assumptions.

## Phase 6 — Advanced Product

- Objective: add persistent and assisted experiences only after demand and operating capacity are demonstrated.
- Deliverables: possible user accounts, saved calculations, personalised dashboards, AI-assisted tools, and APIs.
- Exit criteria: clear user need, privacy/security architecture, data lifecycle, abuse controls, observability, and support ownership.
- Dependencies: authentication, database, security, privacy, operational budget, and product validation.
- Out of scope: treating AI output as regulated advice or storing personal data without explicit safeguards.
